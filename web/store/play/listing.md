## Full description (max 4000)

GaggiMate is the companion app for espresso machines upgraded with a GaggiMate controller. It connects directly to your machine over your home Wi-Fi — no account and no cloud. Your machine's data stays on your network unless you choose to upload a shot to Visualizer.

Your machine at a glance
• Live boiler temperature, pressure, flow and weight while you brew
• Real-time shot chart with targets for temperature, pressure and flow
• Switch between brew, steam and hot water, and start shots from your phone

Profiles
• Browse, select and edit brew profiles
• Pressure and flow profiling on machines with pressure control
• Mark favorites for quick access on the machine's display

Shot history and analysis
• Every shot is logged with its full curves
• Compare and analyze shots in the Shot Analyzer
• Statistics on your brewing over time

Notifications
• Machine preheated and ready to brew
• Shot started and shot finished, with time and weight
• Steam mode left on
• Warnings and errors, like a low water tank

Multiple machines
• Machines on your network are found automatically
• Save several machines and switch between them in one tap

Device settings
• Adjust temperatures, brew and steam behavior, scales, display and Wi-Fi settings of your machine

The app requires a GaggiMate controller installed in your espresso machine and a phone on the same Wi-Fi network. Learn more and get a kit at gaggimate.eu.
